Part 1 · Orientation

1 · How seat licensing is organised

Your organization holds a commercial license. That license carries three things: a seat pool of a fixed size, a set of Florida counties it unlocks (all 67), and an expiration date. As the account admin you hand out seats from that pool to individual people, one seat per email address.

TermPlain meaning
Commercial licenseYour organization’s entitlement record. You may hold more than one.
Seat pack / pool sizeHow many people can be seated at once. One of 5, 10, 15, 30.
SeatOne license assignment to one email address, with a role.
RoleThe access level for that seat — view-only up to full GIS production. See §6.
Active / InvitedA seat’s status. Active = the person has signed in; Invited = assigned, not yet signed in. Both count against the pool.
Account adminWhoever may buy seats and assign / remove users for the license.

Scope An admin manages only the license(s) issued to their own organization. Another company’s seats are never visible or editable. If you sign in and the Seat Licenses screen is absent, your account is not an admin — ask your license holder.

3 · Finding the Seat Licenses screen

Two routes, one destination:

  1. Route A — from the top bar. Click Manage Team & API Keys. In the window that opens, click the Seat Licenses tab.
  2. Route B — from Settings. Open Settings (the gear icon) → Commercial Licensing → click the blue Manage Seats & Users button. This drops you on the same Seat Licenses tab.

What you see on the screen, top to bottom: the selected Commercial License (with a drop-down if you hold several) · the Basic Seat Pack size buttons and a usage bar · four summary cards (seats in use, available, term expiry, county modules) · the Assign a Seat form · the Assigned Seats roster · the activity log.

Part 2 · Administrator tasks

4 · Set or change your seat pack size

The pack size is the ceiling on how many people you can seat. Change it whenever your team grows or shrinks.

  1. Open the Seat Licenses screen (§3) and confirm the right license is selected in the drop-down.
  2. Look at the four size buttons under Basic Seat Pack: 5 / 10 / 15 / 30 Seats. The current size is highlighted. A button is disabled if that size is below your seats currently in use — hover it to see how many seats to revoke first.
    Also shown: “N assigned · M available” and a usage bar.
  3. Click the size you want. A secure checkout page opens (a new tab or redirect).
  4. Confirm the quantity and pay. Billing is per seat, per month. Moving between sizes is prorated automatically — you pay or are credited only the difference for the remainder of the billing period.
  5. Return to the app and click Reload at the top of the screen. The new size and available count are shown.
You cannot shrink below your active seats A 15-seat pack with 12 people seated cannot drop to 10 until you revoke at least 2. The 5 and 10 buttons stay disabled until then.
If checkout didn’t open Your browser blocked the redirect / pop-up. Allow it for the site and click the size button again.

5 · Add a user (assign a seat)

This is the core task. One seat, one email address.

  1. Open the Seat Licenses screen (§3). Confirm the right license in the drop-down, and check the available card shows at least 1. If it shows 0, go to §9 first.
  2. In the “Assign a Seat to a Company User” box, enter the email address the person will sign in with. Use their real work address — it is the identity for the seat.
  3. Enter the full name (optional but recommended — it labels their row in the roster).
  4. Choose a role from the drop-down. It defaults to Basic User. See §6 to pick.
  5. Click Assign Seat.

On success you’ll see a green confirmation (“Seat assigned to … — N of M seats remaining”), the roster gains a row, and the available count drops by one. Behind the scenes the person’s account is created (or updated) with your license’s 67 counties and expiration date, and an email with sign-in instructions goes to them.

Special cases the system handles for you

SituationResult
You assign an email that already has an active seatThe name and role are updated. No extra seat is used.
You assign an email whose seat was revoked earlierThe seat is reactivated (as long as one is available).
The license is domain-bound and the email is outside that domainRejected with “This license is bound to @… — <email> is outside the licensed domain.” Use a work address at the bound domain.
The email is malformedRejected with “A valid user email is required.”
All seats are in useAssign Seat is disabled; a forced attempt returns “All N seats… are in use.”
Adding people from the Team tab The Team & RBAC tab’s Invite Member form also consumes a seat when a seat license is loaded, and is blocked at the cap the same way. Use whichever screen you prefer — the seat accounting is identical.

6 · Choosing the right role

The role decides what the seated person can do. It can be changed later with no seat cost.

RoleGive it to…They get
ViewerReviewers, execs, external stakeholdersRead-only map canvas, zoning matrix, basic parcel info
Basic User
(default)		Most staffMap & GIS layers, parcel attributes, tax metadata
AppraiserValuation & feasibilitySite-feasibility & PDF reports, statewide zoning matrix, tax data
DeveloperSite & subdivision planningSetback envelopes, lot-split calculator, parcel geometry
Senior AnalystFull GIS / survey productionCOGO subdivisions & setbacks, custom cadastre & PDF export, ESRI REST queries, team messaging

Rule of thumb: start people at Basic User; move them up only when a specific tool (reports, setbacks, COGO) is needed for their work.

7 · Change a user’s role

  1. Open the Seat Licenses screen and find the person in the Assigned Seats roster.
  2. Open the role drop-down in their row and pick the new level.
  3. It saves the moment you choose — no button, no seat used. The change is written to the activity log.

The person’s access updates on their next screen load; they don’t need to sign out.

8 · Remove a user

There are two removal actions. Pick based on whether the person is leaving for good.

8a — Revoke a seat (frees the seat, keeps the account)

  1. Open the Seat Licenses screen, find the person in the Assigned Seats roster.
  2. Click Revoke on their row.
  3. The seat is freed immediately — your available count rises by one and you can reassign it.

The person’s account is not deleted. They drop to view-only (Free tier); their saved work is untouched. Re-assign them a seat later and their access returns.

8b — Delete the account (permanent)

  1. Open Manage Team & API Keys → the Team & RBAC tab.
  2. Find the person and click Delete.
  3. The account is removed. If they still held a seat, that seat is freed too.
Deletion cannot be undone. Use Revoke unless the person has truly left.

9 · Handle “the pack is full”

When every seat is in use, the Assign Seat button is disabled and the box reads “Pack is full — revoke a seat or choose a larger pack.”

  1. Review the roster. Anyone who has left, changed roles, or no longer needs access?
  2. If yes → Revoke those seats (§8a). This is free and immediate.
  3. If no → enlarge the pack (§4): pick 10, 15, or 30 and complete the prorated checkout.
  4. Return and assign the new user (§5).

10 · Work with more than one license

If your organization holds several commercial licenses (for example a 15-seat Basic pack and a separate enterprise license), the Commercial License control at the top of the screen becomes a drop-down listing each one, with its plan name and seat usage.

  1. Pick a license from the drop-down.
  2. Everything below — pack size, summary cards, assign form, roster, activity log — now refers to that license only.
  3. Switch any time. Assignments always land on the license shown in the drop-down, so check it before you click Assign Seat.

12 · Import an offline license file

Only if you were issued a signed .lic file instead of buying online.

  1. Open Settings → Commercial Licensing → click Import License (.lic).
  2. Paste the full license text (JSON) into the box.
  3. Click Verify & Activate License and read the result:
    • “License applied. HMAC signature verified.” — trusted and active. Done.
    • “License applied” (without verification) — loaded, but the signature could not be checked on this server. Fine for evaluation; contact support@parceliq.example if you were sent a signed file.
    • An error (“Signature does not match…”, “not valid JSON…”, “missing licenseId…”) — the license was not applied. Re-copy the file exactly, or request a fresh license.
Part 3 · For the assigned user

13 · The seat lifecycle

14 · Signing in & what you can access

  1. Check your email for the seat confirmation. It names your role and the counties unlocked.
  2. Go to band72.github.io/parceliq-site and sign in with the exact email address the seat was assigned to. A typo in the assignment means you can’t sign in — tell your admin to check the roster.
  3. Use the tools for your role (§6). Your access runs through the license’s expiration date.
  4. If your seat is later removed, your login still works but drops to a read-only view. Nothing you saved is lost. Ask your admin to re-seat you to restore full access.

17 · Glossary

Active seatA seat whose user has signed in at least once.
Commercial licenseYour organization’s entitlement record; holds the seat pool, counties and expiry.
Domain bindingAn optional restriction limiting seats to email addresses at one domain.
Invited seatA seat that has been assigned but whose user hasn’t signed in yet. Still counts against the pool.
Pack / pool sizeThe maximum number of concurrent seats: 5, 10, 15 or 30.
ProrationBilling only the difference for the remainder of the period when you change pack size mid-cycle.
ProvisioningThe automatic setup of a user’s account (counties, expiry, email) when you assign them a seat.
RevokeFree a seat; the user drops to view-only but keeps their account.
RoleThe access level attached to a seat: Viewer, Basic User, Appraiser, Developer, Senior Analyst.
SeatOne license assignment to one email address.
