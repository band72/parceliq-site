Capabilities

Tools are modular plugins on a shared map, so a parcel you pick in one tool is already loaded in the rest.

Search & public records

  • Statewide property search by address, owner, parcel ID or map extent, with tax-roll filters.
  • Deed & plat search by book, page and instrument number across LandmarkWeb, MyFloridaCounty, Tyler Eagle and AcclaimWeb clerk portals.
  • Deed reader that pulls legal descriptions and checks metes-and-bounds closure.
  • Chain of title and county clerk / appraiser directory.
  • Quit-claim deed form with legal-description, map and hold-harmless exhibits.
  • Tax deed auction screener comparing assessed value with opening bids.

Survey & CAD

  • COGO suite: Bowditch traverse balancing and closure checks against Florida's Standards of Practice (Fla. Admin. Code 5J-17).
  • DXF & LandXML export of parcel linework for AutoCAD / Civil 3D.
  • GeoCoord inspector: DD, DMS and Florida State Plane easting/northing in US survey feet and meters.
  • PLSS overlay: section, township, range and Spanish land grants, with quarter and quarter-quarter aliquot parts.
  • Parcel split assistant that drafts lot layouts and their legal descriptions.
  • Measure, sketch and georeferenced plat, tax-panel and topo-map layers.

Flood & environment

  • FEMA flood zones, base flood elevations and FIRM panels from the National Flood Hazard Layer.
  • Elevation Certificate studio for FEMA Form FF-206-FY-22-152.
  • Flood elevation engineering: BFE + county freeboard = finished-floor elevation.
  • LiDAR topography: bare-earth elevations, 1 ft contours and NAVD88 ↔ NGVD29 conversion from USGS 3DEP.
  • Wetlands (USFWS NWI, Cowardin classes, upland ratio), wells and fire hydrant proximity.

Planning & feasibility

  • Zoning & setbacks drawn on the parcel, with height, FAR and lot-coverage limits where the county publishes them.
  • House-plan fitter to test a footprint against the buildable envelope.
  • Live Local Act (SB 102) screener for multifamily density, height and tax-exemption eligibility.
  • Site research report combining flood, wetlands, zoning and records into one summary.
  • Batch address processing and printable property packages.

No synthetic data. When an official source has no value for a field, ParcelIQ shows it as unverified instead of filling in a typical number.

Platform

A browser map client and an API server, backed by a spatial database. Public agency services are read at query time; county records are linked back to their official source.

Spatial database

Parcels are stored in PostgreSQL with PostGIS geometry in WGS 84 (EPSG:4326), with a GiST spatial index for map-extent queries and vector tiles. Zoning standards for Florida jurisdictions sit in their own table, joined to parcels by zoning code.

The current proof of concept runs on a local PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS instance seeded with real Duval County parcel polygons, the same schema the hosted service uses.

Data sources

  • County property appraisers & Florida DOR tax roll
  • County clerk official records (deeds, plats)
  • FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer
  • USGS 3DEP elevation
  • USFWS National Wetlands Inventory
  • BLM PLSS & Florida land grants
  • Florida DEP / WMD well and permit data
